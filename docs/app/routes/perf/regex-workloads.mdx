# Regex Workloads

The general regex comparison uses identifiable production patterns and explicit
operation contracts. It separates basic expressions, complex expressions and
advanced Oniguruma features. TextMate Scanner replays and Ferriki's complete
highlighting have separate workload boundaries.

The new corpus and runner are ready for measurement. This page documents their
scope; it makes no new performance claim. Superseded measurement pages and
artifacts are available through the repository's Git history.

These are isolated regex steps. They do not time the complete validators,
parsers, templates, entity extraction or rendering applications. The 513
upstream-derived inputs establish expected semantics, not a production traffic
distribution. Four additional literal probes vary match position explicitly on
a controlled derivative of a real log record.

Linkify-it's UTF-16 HTTP-tail expression and PHP-CS-Fixer's recursive type
grammar remain documented candidates. The former needs a faithful native UTF-8
translation. The latter already exposes a consumed recursive-capture difference;
whole-match agreement would not be enough to admit it.

## Comparable work before comparable speed

An engine must reproduce every fixture's defined output before timing: boolean,
first span, captures with unset groups preserved, all captures, replacement string
or grapheme chunks. Missing syntax, semantic differences, execution errors and
timeouts have separate statuses. A missing result is not a slow timing or a
negative match.

Patterns share one documented dialect recipe. Python strict-end anchors,
ECMAScript ASCII digits and PHP ASCII word classes are explicit. Match-at-offset
preserves the complete subject when lookbehind needs preceding context. Future
alternative solutions to the same task need their own profile and must include
necessary preprocessing and output work.

Ferroni uses its public `Regex` API. The comparators are C Oniguruma, pinned Ruby
Onigmo, PCRE2 interpreter/JIT, fancy-regex in Oniguruma mode with seek off/on,
and Rust regex. Construction includes each profile's contexts and selected JIT.
PCRE2's bundled source is pinned through the locked `pcre2-sys` dependency.
Replacement/output assembly stays within the operation boundary.

## Measurement and interpretation

Compile, compile-plus-first-use and steady reuse are separate lanes. First use
concerns the first fixture, while steady reuse executes the complete fixture
batch. These are fresh-pattern costs in a warmed process; they do not measure
OS-process startup. Memory, where selected, is whole-process peak RSS including
runtime, catalog, allocator and output, rather than isolated pattern storage.

The runner starts with three independent processes per pair, 30 Criterion
samples per lane and three seconds measurement. It rotates/reverses engine order,
keeps raw samples and intervals, and reports inconsistent differences as
inconclusive. Short diagnostic runs cannot establish publication claims. Review
host conditions and stability before interpreting a publication candidate.

Each comparison lists its exact common tasks. Families carry equal aggregate
weight regardless of fixture or variant count. Aggregates with different supported
task pools do not establish an engine ranking. ARM and x86 results are reported
separately.

The [corpus guide](https://github.com/sebastian-software/ferroni/blob/main/benches/regex_corpus.md)
contains runnable commands, the earlier patterns' dispositions, measured
boundaries, current coverage limits and the requirements for adding cases.
